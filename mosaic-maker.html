Before you order
  • Screen colors are approximate. Order a printed ORACAL 651 color chart and hold it next to your picture, then fix any color in step 3.
  • Craft sellers usually sell 12″ × 10 ft rolls. Sign-supply distributors sell 10-yard (30 ft) and 50-yard (150 ft) rolls in 12″, 15″, 24″ and wider. Big jobs are usually cheapest in 10- or 50-yard rolls.
  • For a school purchase order or bulk pricing, buy through an authorized ORAFOL sign-supply distributor. ORAFOL Americas' sales team can point you to one near you.
  • Many colors also come in matte (marked below), which cuts glare on a wall under bright lights.
